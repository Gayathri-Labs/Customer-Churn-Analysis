# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn using PostgreSQL and Power BI. The goal is to identify customer behavior, understand the factors behind churn, and present the findings through an interactive dashboard.

---

## Tools Used

- PostgreSQL
- Power BI
- DAX

---

## Dataset

- Total Records: 1,000
- Source: Customer Churn Dataset (CSV)

---

## Project Workflow

1. Imported the CSV dataset into PostgreSQL.
2. Cleaned and validated the data.
3. Wrote SQL queries to answer business questions.
4. Connected PostgreSQL to Power BI.
5. Created DAX measures for KPIs.
6. Built an interactive dashboard.
7. Identified key business insights and recommendations.

---

## Business Questions

1. How many total customers are there?
2. How many customers have churned?
3. What is the average age of customers?
4. What is the average account balance?
5. How many active and inactive members are there?
6. Which gender has the highest churn rate?
7. What is the average balance of churned customers?
8. Does the number of products owned affect customer churn?
9. Which age group has the highest churn rate?
10. What is the average tenure of customers who churned?
11. Are inactive members more likely to churn than active members?
12. Is there any relationship between credit card ownership and churn?
13. Which customer segment (based on age and balance) has the highest churn?
14. What percentage of total customers have churned?
15. Identify high-value customers (high balance) who are at risk of churn.

---

## Key Insights

- Most customers are retained.
- Female customers have a slightly higher churn rate.
- Inactive customers are more likely to churn.
- Customers with more products show a higher chance of churn.
- Middle-aged customers have a higher churn tendency.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Improve customer retention strategies.
- Increase engagement with inactive customers.
- Monitor high-value customers closely.
- Offer personalized promotions to customers at risk of churn.
- Focus on customer segments with higher churn rates.
